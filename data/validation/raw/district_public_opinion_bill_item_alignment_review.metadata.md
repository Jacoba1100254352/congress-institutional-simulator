# District Public-Opinion Bill Item Alignment Review Metadata

Curated source review of the 22 current district public-opinion acquisition packets against official GovInfo BILLSTATUS titles and latest CRS summaries plus the current Cumulative CES Policy Preferences candidate items.

- Unit: one bill review packet.
- Source-reviewed rows: 22.
- Selected historical issue-item alignments: 1.
- Review method: compare the enacted bill's official title and latest CRS summary with the survey item's official guide description and response direction.
- Negative dispositions are retained so broad policy-area similarity is not mistaken for a bill-text match.
- Raw respondent records and respondent identifiers are not retained.

Claim boundary: source-reviewed bill-to-item disposition only. A selected item supplies historical issue-support context rather than exact bill support. It does not provide MRP, contemporaneous district opinion, affected-group support or harm, public benefit, causal representation, or model validation.
