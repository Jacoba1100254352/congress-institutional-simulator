Each published row joins an official Cumulative CES policy-preference response to annual CCES Common Content geography and validated-voter weights, then reports only a district aggregate that meets the minimum respondent threshold. The survey item is directionally related to the bill but is not the bill wording, and the estimate predates enactment.
